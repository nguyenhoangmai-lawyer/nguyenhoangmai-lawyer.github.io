About

Nguyen Hoang Mai is a Lawyer and Senior Lecturer at the Faculty of Labour Relations and Trade Unions, Trade Union University, Hanoi, Viet Nam.

Her academic and professional work focuses on labour relations, collective bargaining, labour law, trade unions, worker representation and voice, workplace social dialogue, labour governance, and the institutional development of employment relations in Viet Nam.

Her current research also examines the implications of digital transformation for labour relations, including platform work, algorithmic management, workers' digital rights, data governance, artificial intelligence, and sustainable work.

Alongside research and university teaching, she is engaged in professional training, academic advising, labour-relations education, and capacity building for trade union officials, workers' representatives, managers and practitioners.

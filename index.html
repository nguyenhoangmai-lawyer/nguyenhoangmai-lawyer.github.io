Academic Profile

Nguyen Hoang Mai is a Senior Lecturer at the Faculty of Labour Relations and Trade Unions, Trade Union University, Hanoi, and a jurist engaged in labour and trade-union law. Her teaching, research and professional work centres on labour relations, collective bargaining, labour law, trade unions, worker representation and voice, workplace social dialogue, labour governance and international labour standards.

Her current research extends these themes to digital transformation, platform work, algorithmic management, labour-data governance, workers’ digital rights and sustainable labour governance. She also contributes to professional training and capacity building for trade union officials, workers’ representatives, managers, practitioners and students.

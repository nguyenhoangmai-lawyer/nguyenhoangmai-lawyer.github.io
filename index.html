Academic Profile

Hồ sơ học thuật

Nguyen Hoang Mai (Nguyễn Hoàng Mai) is a Lawyer, Senior Lecturer and researcher at the Faculty of Labour Relations and Trade Unions, Trade Union University, Hanoi, Viet Nam.

Her principal academic fields are labour relations, labour law, collective bargaining, trade unions, worker representation, social dialogue, labour policy and international labour standards.

She has been engaged in research and teaching at Trade Union University since 2001 and has also worked as a lawyer providing consultation, professional guidance and training in labour and trade union law.

Her recent research extends to digital transformation in labour relations, platform work, algorithmic management, workers' digital rights, data governance and sustainable labour governance.
